A 54-year-old patient was diagnosed with Stage III breast cancer in January 2021.
Neoadjuvant chemotherapy was started in February 2021.
Surgical resection was performed in June 2021.
Adjuvant radiotherapy was completed in September 2021.
Restaging imaging in March 2022 showed a partial response with the tumor reduced in size.
A surveillance visit in March 2023 showed stable disease.
