A 62-year-old patient was diagnosed with Stage III colorectal cancer in June 2020.
Surgical resection was performed in July 2020.
Adjuvant chemotherapy was started in September 2020.
Restaging imaging in June 2021 showed no evidence of disease.
Serum CEA was elevated in December 2021.
Imaging in February 2022 confirmed new liver metastases indicating disease progression.
Second-line chemotherapy was started in March 2022.
